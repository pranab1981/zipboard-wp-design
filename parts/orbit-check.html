How it works

Three steps, and a person decides every one.

  1. 11

    Pick the check

    Run the standard editorial review, or write your own rules: your terminology, your style guide, what this document must and must not say.

  2. 22

    Orbit reads the PDF

    Orbit works through the document in zipBoard’s PDF viewer and lists what it finds, before the file goes out to your reviewers.

  3. 33

    You triage the findings

    Accept a finding and it becomes a task with an owner, tracked with the rest of the review. Dismiss it and it is gone. The asset shows how many were accepted, dismissed and still to triage.

Know the limits

What Orbit Check is, and what it isn’t.

PDFs today
Orbit Check works on PDFs. Web pages can be reviewed and compared side by side, but not checked by Orbit yet.
A first pass, not a sign-off
Orbit finds candidates. Your reviewers still review, and only people approve.
Nothing changes on its own
Every finding waits for a person to accept or dismiss it. Accepted findings are ordinary tasks.
