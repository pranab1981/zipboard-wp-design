How a review moves

Know exactly where every review stands.

  1. Sent for review

    Send for review

    Upload a version, pick reviewers and a due date. Everyone gets an email. Nobody needs an account to start.

  2. Seen

    See who has opened it

    “Seen” is recorded automatically, so you know who is engaged and who still needs a nudge.

  3. Commented

    Comment in context

    Pins, shapes, highlights and @mentions land on the asset itself. Content owners resolve, reply or reject.

  4. Approved

    Approve with confidence

    Each reviewer marks Approved or Feedback shared. The round closes when everyone who matters has signed off.
